Claims Reality

Why claims matter in motor insurance

Motor claims in Kenya are often delayed by poor documentation at the scene — no photos, no police abstract, missing third-party details. Insurers cannot assess what they cannot verify, and drivers lose weeks chasing paperwork after the fact.

ADT provides a pre-claim checklist drivers can carry in their vehicle and a WhatsApp claims line available around the clock for incident logging. We tell you what to capture before vehicles move and file with the insurer once your file is complete.

Quick Guide

Three steps after a motor incident

01

Secure the scene
