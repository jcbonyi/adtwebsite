Motor FAQ

Common motor insurance questions

What documents do I need for a motor quote?

Typically logbook copy, driver details, prior policy information (if any), and vehicle usage summary. Your advisor will confirm based on cover type.

Can ADT support fleet renewals on one schedule?

Yes. We consolidate fleet records, renewal dates, and claims trends so multi-vehicle operators can manage cover efficiently.

How fast can I report a motor claim?

Contact the claims desk immediately via phone or WhatsApp. Our team targets first response within 15 minutes during operating hours.
