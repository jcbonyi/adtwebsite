SME Risk

The insurance risks SMEs in Kenya typically underestimate

Many SMEs undervalue stock on policy schedules, discover WIBA non-compliance only after an inspection, or assume a standard fire policy covers lost revenue during downtime. A personal motor policy rarely extends to delivery bikes or sales vehicles used daily for business.

These gaps surface at the worst time — during a claim or regulatory review. ADT helps you right-size cover before an incident, with one advisor who understands how your business actually operates.
