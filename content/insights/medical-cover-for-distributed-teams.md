Distributed teams need medical plans that balance inpatient, outpatient, and emergency support access.

Map employee location patterns and provider access before finalizing benefit structures.

Align waiting periods and benefit limits with your workforce profile to avoid gaps at critical moments.

Review usage trends quarterly and adjust plan design as team size and operating footprint change.
