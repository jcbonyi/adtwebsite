---
title: Corporate Insurance Review Checklist for Expanding Operations
category: Corporate Advisory
author: ADT Corporate Risk Desk
publishedAt: 2026-05-10
excerpt: A practical annual checklist for enterprises reviewing liability, asset, and continuity insurance structures.
---

Start with your current risk register and compare it against active policies.

Review policy limits against inflation, asset additions, and cross-border operating exposure.

Check whether board governance requirements and contractual insurance clauses are reflected in policy wording.

Track claims patterns from the past 12 months and use lessons to improve policy structure before renewal.
