As your team grows, liability exposure grows with it.

WIBA should be reviewed alongside employers liability and public liability requirements.

Document role-based risk exposures and update policy declarations at renewal to avoid underinsurance.

A proactive compliance review reduces penalties, claim friction, and financial uncertainty.
