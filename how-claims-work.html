The ADT claims workflow

Claims Process

  1. ReportNotify us by WhatsApp, phone, or online form. We log your case and prioritise urgent incidents.
  2. DocumentWe guide your team on required documents to reduce back-and-forth and speed insurer review.
  3. SubmitADT files with the insurer, confirms receipt, and tracks assessor appointments on your behalf.
  4. ResolveWe follow up with insurers and service providers while sharing clear status updates until settlement.

FAQs

Claims process questions

How fast can ADT respond to a new claim?

Our team targets first response within 15 minutes during operating hours, with follow-up guidance provided immediately after review.

What documents do I need for a motor claim?

Typically a police abstract, driver's licence, logbook, photos of damage, and any third-party details. Download our claim checklist for a full list by claim type.

Can I report a claim through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can report claims and receive status updates through our dedicated WhatsApp support channel at any time.

Ready to start a claim?

Our claims desk will log your case and guide required documentation from the first contact.
