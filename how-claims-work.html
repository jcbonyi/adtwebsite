Claims Guide

How claims work with ADT

Our process is designed for speed, transparency, and practical support from first report to settlement updates.

Step 1: Immediate report

Notify us by WhatsApp, phone, or online form. We log your case and prioritize urgent incidents quickly.

Step 2: Document readiness

We guide your team on required documents to reduce back-and-forth and speed insurer review.

Step 3: Insurer coordination

ADT follows up with insurers, assessors, and service providers while sharing clear status updates with you.
