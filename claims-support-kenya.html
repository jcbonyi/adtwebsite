Claims Process

How we support your claim

Claims Support Workflow

  1. ReportCall or WhatsApp our claims desk immediately after the incident.
  2. DocumentWe tell you exactly which documents to collect at the scene.
  3. SubmitWe file with the insurer and confirm receipt on your behalf.
  4. ResolveWe follow up until settlement is confirmed and communicated to you.

Preparation

Before you call — gather these if you can

✓ Your policy number or insurer name

✓ Date, time, and location of the incident

✓ Photos of damage (as many angles as possible)

✓ Third-party contact details (for motor incidents)

✓ Police abstract reference number (where applicable)

✓ Any relevant receipts or invoices for damaged items
