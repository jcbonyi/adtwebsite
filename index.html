FAQs

Common questions, answered clearly

How fast can ADT respond to a new claim?

Our team targets first response within 15 minutes during operating hours, with follow-up guidance provided immediately after review.

Do you support SME and logistics businesses?

Yes. We specialize in SME, transport, and logistics risk profiles, including motor, goods in transit, liability, and WIBA cover.

Can I request support through WhatsApp?

Yes. You can request quotes, report claims, and receive updates through our dedicated WhatsApp support channel.

Do you support multi-country operations?

Yes. We support Kenyan businesses with regional and global operations, coordinating with suitable underwriter networks for cross-border exposures.

Can ADT help us review policy fit before renewal?

Yes. We provide pre-renewal advisory and claims trend reviews to improve policy fit and reduce surprise gaps.
