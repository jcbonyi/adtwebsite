Corporate FAQ

Questions corporate buyers ask

Can ADT manage multiple policies across locations?

Yes. We consolidate schedules, renewal dates, and claims records so multi-site teams have a single advisory point of contact.

Do you support pre-renewal reviews?

Yes. We run structured renewal reviews covering assets, liability, workforce cover, and claims trends before bind.

How do you handle claims escalation for corporate accounts?

Named claims contacts, documentation quality checks, and regular insurer follow-up until resolution or settlement guidance is complete.

Can you align cover with contractual requirements?

We review client contracts, landlord requirements, and tender obligations to ensure limits and extensions match your exposure.
