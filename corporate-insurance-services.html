Corporate Risk

What corporate risk looks like

Corporate clients face layered exposures — property, liability, workforce, supply chain, and business continuity — that rarely fit a single off-the-shelf policy. Growth, new locations, and changing headcounts can leave critical gaps between renewal cycles.

ADT maps how your operations actually run before recommending cover, so limits, endorsements, and schedules reflect real exposure rather than generic templates.

Core Covers

Core corporate covers

Property & Fire Insurance

Protect buildings, equipment, stock, and fixtures against fire, theft, and structural loss. Covers head office and branch locations.

Public & Product Liability

Coverage for third-party injury, property damage, and product liability claims. Essential for client-facing and manufacturing operations.

Employers' Liability / WIBA

Mandatory workforce injury compensation in compliance with the Work Injury Benefits Act. Includes employee schedules and renewal reminders.

Business Interruption

Covers lost revenue and fixed operating costs when operations are disrupted by fire, flood, or other covered events.

Professional Indemnity

Protects against claims arising from professional advice, errors, or omissions. Critical for consultancies, engineers, and service firms.

Asset & Machinery Insurance

Plant, machinery, electronics, and operational assets covered against breakdown, theft, and accidental damage.

FAQs

Corporate insurance questions

What is the minimum size for corporate insurance advisory?

We support businesses of all sizes. Corporate advisory typically applies to organisations with 10+ employees, multiple assets, or liability exposure that requires structured policy review rather than off-the-shelf cover.

Can ADT coordinate cover across multiple business locations?

Yes. We map coverage across head office, branches, warehouses, and field operations — ensuring no location or asset class is inadvertently uninsured.

How does ADT support us during a corporate claim?

We provide a named claims contact, assist with document preparation, file directly with the underwriter, and provide structured follow-up until settlement is reached.
